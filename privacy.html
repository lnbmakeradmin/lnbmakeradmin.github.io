Our free Services may display ads. Ad partners may use your advertising ID and usage data to show ads that match your interests. You can control this at any time:

  • iOS: if you do not allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, your IDFA is not used. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • EEA/UK: we show a consent screen as required by law. If you do not consent, you will only see non-personalized ads.
  • You can learn more or opt out at aboutads.info (US), youronlinechoices.eu (EU) and youradchoices.ca (Canada).
